The Weight Rack: Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

Who we are

The Weight Rack is an app that plans and tracks weight-training workouts. Questions about your data: use Feedback in the app, or email the address in the TestFlight invitation.

What we collect

Your email address and a password (the password is handled by our sign-in provider and is never visible to us in readable form). Profile details you choose to enter: name, age, gender, height, weight, phone number, mailing address, your goal, experience level, and training preferences. Workout data: your plans, sets, weights, reps, notes, the gyms and equipment you set up, and the feedback you send us.

How we use it

To build and adjust your workouts, show your history and stats, and improve the app and its workout engine. We also study the equipment in home gyms as plain anonymous lists, with no name or street address attached (only the city and state you entered, if any), to learn what a typical home gym holds. We do not sell your data and we do not show ads. The app has no third-party analytics or advertising tools.

Where it is stored

Your data is stored with our backend provider, Supabase, which hosts it on our behalf. It is sent over encrypted connections.

On your device

If you turn on Face ID, your sign-in is kept in the iPhone’s secure keychain and unlocked with Face ID. We never receive your face data; it stays with iOS. If you allow Apple Music access, the app only sends play, pause and skip commands.

Sharing

We do not share your personal data with other people. Trainers or gym owners you are connected to in the app may see the plans and workouts you share with them. We may disclose data if the law requires it.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time in Settings → Account Settings → Delete My Account. That erases your profile, contact details, plans, preferences, benchmarks and login. Your training history (which exercises were done, and the weights, reps and dates of each set) is kept in anonymous form so the app can learn how to build better workouts for everyone: it is moved to a shared system account, it is no longer linked to your name, email or anything else that identifies you, and any notes you wrote on it are erased. If you set up a home gym, the list of equipment in it is kept the same way, as a plain list with no name or street address, together with the city and state you entered (if any). Feedback you sent us is kept the same way, without your identity.

Children

The app is not intended for children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change.
